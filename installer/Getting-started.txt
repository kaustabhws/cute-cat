Cute Cat

Open Cute Cat from the Start menu or desktop shortcut. Click the cat to greet it, drag to move it, or right-click for its menu. Closing the control window keeps the cat in the tray; choose Quit to stop it.

In Quiet desktop, enable the optional Windows notification paw helper. Reduced motion, a locked desktop and full-screen suppression can pause it. This installer preserves existing preferences and does not enable Windows startup automatically.

To uninstall: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Cute Cat > Uninstall.
Uninstall closes Cute Cat, removes installed program files and shortcuts, and removes certificate trust owned by this installation. Your settings and focus history remain in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CuteCat for a later reinstall.

In App guard, add an open app or browse to its .exe, choose reminder/paw-close and when it applies, then enable the guard. No apps are selected automatically. Save prompts remain under your control. You can pause the guard from the modern pet/tray menu.

Your cat has bandana, bow tie, bell collar and flower accessories, several colours, activity levels, and optional idle naps. Idle naps wake with a stretch when you return; manual Sleep waits for Wake or interaction. Animations remain entirely code-driven.

Use Profiles to choose Work, Study or Break, set activity and schedule weekly changes. Selecting a profile manually pauses schedules until you enable them again. The global app guard switch always stays under your control.

Each app rule can have a grace period, a daily allowance and a temporary exception. Only selected-app foreground totals are stored locally; no titles or browsing timeline are recorded. Drag the cat to remember a spot on that monitor. Settings also offers optional focused-control avoidance.

Settings > Updates & recovery checks GitHub for previews and verifies the installer before opening setup. Downloads and installation require your action. Setup retains this and the previous compatible signed installer in the Recovery folder for repair/rollback. This release establishes the recovery baseline; the older unsigned installer is not used for verified rollback.

This preview's notification-access certificate expires on @@EXPIRY@@. Setup and its uninstaller are signed and timestamped with the same protected preview identity. It is not public publisher verification.

This is a local development build. Browser feed protection remains future work.
